# Accreditations (and) Other Business Papers

### A project aimed to streamline the process of creating certificates for large groups of people by utilizing Excel spreadsheets

# How to use:

1. Select a document template.
2. Select an Excel spreadsheet to pull data from (must be unprotected .xls).
3. Select any of the provided selections (they are the first row of your document's first sheet) and press "Add selection to image".
4. Drag the field onto an image (automatically follows your mouse).
5. Click when you are satisfied with the field's position.
6. Repeat steps 3-5 until all fields that you need on the final document are pasted in.
7. Press the "Save images" button, select your destination.
8. A new folder named "Output" will be created in the destination directory, and all the images rendered will be put there.
9. Enjoy your created accreditations and other business papers.
