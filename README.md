# Accreditations (and) Other Business Papers

### A program for quickly and easily putting text on images such as certificates in large amounts

# Features:

* Fast image and document import
* Vast support for different images (.png, .jpeg., .bmp and others) and documents (.xlsx, .xls, .ods, .csv and others)
* Basic text customization features (text centering, text color, etc.)
* Intuitive UI

# How to use:

1. Select a document template.
2. Select a document to pull selections from (must be unprotected).
3. Select any of the selections generated by the program (the first row of your document's first sheet) and press "Add selection to image".
4. Drag the placeholder onto the image (it automatically follows your mouse).
5. Click when you are satisfied with the placeholder's position.
6. Repeat steps 3-5 until all fields that you need on the final document are pasted in.
7. Press the "Save images" button, select your destination.
8. While saving, data corresponding to every placeholder will be pulled from your document and applied to the final image in the positions you put them on.
9. Enjoy your created accreditations and other business papers. :)
